Departmental General Order — Mandatory Felony Custody:

                    
All sworn SASP personnel are strictly prohibited from issuing a citation or cite-and-release for
                        felony offenses. Felony suspects must be arrested and booked through a formal detention
                        facility.

                    

                        ⚠️ Disciplinary Advisory: Issuing a citation for a felony instead of arresting
                        constitutes an unauthorized discharge of duty and will result in Internal Affairs (IA)
                            Disciplinary Actions and administrative sanctions.
